Where India's freight is off normal

For anyone moving goods into, out of or across India. Every morning this page compares India's ports, the Gulf and Asian ports its cargo passes through, the sea routes, the main highways and the customs gateways with their own normal, using public data. Red and amber mark what is running worse than usual. Tap a place for its figures.

New since yesterday

Bengaluru's inland depot is clearing imports at less than half its usual pace

15.6% of 135 import bills cleared within 48 hours on 25 Nov, against a median of 38% since 1 Nov, and 62% took more than three days. It is the third working day in a row under a quarter. Boxes for Peenya and Hosur makers wait days at the depot before they can be trucked out.

CBIC ICEDASH, 25 Nov · data

At Ennore, 21 of 22 import bills took more than three days to clear

Kamarajar port cleared over 90% within 48 hours on 21–23 Nov, then 14% on 24 Nov and 5% on 25 Nov. The count is small, so one importer's paperwork could explain it; a third day like this would make it real. Until then, boxes landing at Ennore may sit longer before leaving the port.
